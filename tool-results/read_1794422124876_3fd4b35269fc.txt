    22→# 1. PRODUCT VISION
    23→
    24→### What is ClientHub?
    25→
    26→ClientHub is the online customer portal for Jeffery Group Ltd.
    27→
    28→Its purpose is to modernize the way Jeffery Group communicates and conducts business with its existing packaging customers.
    29→
    30→Currently, business-to-business packaging orders can involve a considerable amount of manual communication:
    31→
    32→- Customers contact Jeffery Group to request product information.
    33→- Customers ask for pricing.
    34→- Customers email or message their order quantities.
    35→- Employees manually confirm product availability.
    36→- Customers request previous invoices and purchase orders.
    37→- Customers ask for updates about their shipments.
    38→- Employees have to look up historical orders.
    39→- Customers request quotations for customized packaging.
    40→
    41→ClientHub should bring these interactions together into a single, centralized platform.
    42→
    43→Instead of customers having to contact Jeffery Group for every small request, they should be able to log in and access the information they need themselves.
    44→
    45→The platform should make purchasing from Jeffery Group feel professional, easy, transparent, and convenient.
    46→
    47→### The main objectives
    48→
    49→1. Provide customers with a professional online portal.
    50→2. Simplify the purchasing and ordering process.
    51→3. Reduce repetitive administrative work for employees.
    52→4. Allow customers to quickly reorder previous products.
    53→5. Centralize quotations, invoices, and business documents.
    54→6. Improve transparency throughout the order fulfillment process.
    55→7. Give Jeffery Group complete control over customer accounts and negotiated pricing.
    56→8. Create a scalable foundation for future business automation.
    57→
    58→The application should serve as an extension of Jeffery Group's existing business operations.
